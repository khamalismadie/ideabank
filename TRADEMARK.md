# Trademark Policy

## Ideabank™ Trademark Guidelines

### What is covered

- The name "Ideabank"
- The Ideabank logo
- Any official Ideabank branding

### Acceptable Use

You may use the Ideabank name and logo:

- To refer to the Ideabank software itself
- In tutorials, blog posts, and documentation
- In the title of talks and presentations
- To indicate compatibility (e.g., "Works with Ideabank")

### Unacceptable Use

You may NOT use the Ideabank name or logo:

- As part of your company, product, or service name
- In a way that suggests endorsement or affiliation
- For merchandise or commercial products
- In domain names
- To promote unauthorized modifications

### Forks and Derivatives

If you fork or modify Ideabank:

- You must change the name
- You must remove all Ideabank branding
- You may not use "Ideabank" in your derivative's name
- You must include the original LICENSE and copyright notice

### Domain Names

- `ideabank.dev` — Official
- `ideabank.com` — Official
- Any other `ideabank.*` domain is NOT official

### Questions?

If you're unsure whether your use is allowed, open a GitHub Discussion or contact trademark@ideabank.dev.
